Lab Workflow – Test Scenarios (Rewritten Specification)
Scenario 1 — Revisit with Unsent Orders
Flow
Doctor sends lab order → Patient moves to Lab Queue
Lab staff opens patient → Marks lab Complete
Patient returns to Doctor Queue
Doctor adds a new lab order (NOT sent to lab)
Doctor marks Consultation Complete
Patient again appears in Lab Queue
Lab staff opens lab panel
Expected Behavior
Lab panel should show ONLY unsent lab orders
Previously completed lab results should NOT interfere
Patient correctly re-enters lab flow based on new pending orders
Scenario 2 — Manual Void Before Re-Entry
Flow
Doctor sends lab order → Patient goes to Lab Queue
Doctor voids the lab test
Doctor calls patient back from Lab Queue
Doctor creates a new order set
Doctor marks consultation complete
Patient goes to Lab Queue again
Lab staff processes the patient
Expected Behavior
Lab staff should see ONLY unsent (new) orders
❌ Voided orders must NOT appear
Patient progresses normally after completion
Scenario 3 — Void + Auto Cleanup + New Orders
Flow
Doctor sends lab order → Patient goes to Lab Queue
Doctor voids the test
System automatically removes patient from lab workflow
Doctor enters new test order
Doctor completes consultation
Patient returns to Lab Queue
Lab staff processes patient
Expected Behavior
System should auto-void previous sent order (if applicable)
Lab panel must show ONLY new unsent orders
❌ Voided orders must not appear anywhere
Patient continues normal lab flow
Scenario 4 — Mixed State (Void + Sent + Unsent + Completed)
Flow
Doctor sends lab order → Patient goes to Lab Queue
Doctor voids previous test
Doctor creates new lab order
Doctor sends new order to lab
Lab staff sees latest sent-to-lab order only
Lab completes results → Patient returns to Doctor Queue
Doctor adds unsent additional orders
Doctor marks consultation complete
Patient goes back to Lab Queue again
Expected Behavior
Lab staff should see:
✅ Latest Sent to Lab orders
❌ Not voided orders
❌ Not already completed orders
Only active lab orders should appear in lab panel
System must always respect latest valid state only

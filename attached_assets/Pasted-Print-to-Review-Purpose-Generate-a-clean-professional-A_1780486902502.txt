Print to Review

Purpose

Generate a clean, professional, A4-sized PDF version of the patient's health record for review, printing, sharing, and archival purposes.

Dynamic Form Support

The system must not hardcode SOAP sections.

Instead:

Read all configured specialty form sections.
Render sections in configured order.
Skip empty sections.
Support:

Design Requirements
Paper Size
A4 Portrait
Margins
15–20 mm all sides
Typography
Section Headings: 16–18px Bold
Sub Headings: 13–14px Bold
Content: 11–12px
Footer: 9–10px
Styling
Clean healthcare-style design
Professional hospital-grade appearance
Consistent spacing
Light grey section separators
High print readability
Black & white printer friendly
No excessive colours
Proper page breaks between large sections
Additional Requirements
Multi-page support.
Repeat header on every page.
Repeat footer on every page.
No data truncation.
Long notes should automatically continue to next page.
PDF should open in a new browser tab before printing/downloading.
Generated PDF must look suitable for submission to hospitals, insurance companies, referral physicians, and official medical record requests.

The overall appearance should resemble a professionally issued electronic health record rather than a system export or report.
